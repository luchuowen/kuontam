FAQ

Straight answers.

Do I need all five systems?

No. Many clients start with one, usually backup power or CCTV. We design it so the others can be added later without redoing the work.

What happens at a site survey?

We visit, assess your power load and security risks, and agree what you need. You then get drawings and an itemised quotation.

Can you work with my architect or contractor?

Yes. We work alongside clients, consultants and other contractors, and fit into your project programme.

Do you maintain systems after installation?

Yes. After-sales support and maintenance are part of how we work, well beyond project delivery.
